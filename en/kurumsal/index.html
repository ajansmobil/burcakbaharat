Who Are We?

Burçak Baharat

At our facility in the Gaziantep İslahiye Organized Industrial Zone, built on 25,000 square meters of land, 10,000 square meters of which is covered area, we produce in the spice, pastry products, sauce group, beverage group, and industrial group. We source agricultural products such as red pepper, cumin, sesame, nigella seeds, sumac, and mint from local farmers, and we export to 15 countries on different continents.
